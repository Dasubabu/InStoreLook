# InStoreLook — Premium Demo Runbook

## Before the meeting

- Use a desktop browser window at 1440px or wider.
- Have the latest Vercel deployment URL ready.
- Confirm browser location permission is enabled.
- Keep the investor video and this runbook open.
- Do not claim external map businesses have product stock unless they are
  registered InStoreLook sellers.

## 6-minute live demo

### 1. Open with the problem — 45 seconds

Say:

> “Maps can tell a shopper where a store is. Marketplaces can show what ships.
> InStoreLook connects those two questions for local fashion: what is nearby,
> what is available, and what should I do next?”

### 2. Shopper discovery — 90 seconds

1. Choose **Shop Nearby**.
2. Allow location permission.
3. Point out the detected Indian city.
4. Show nearby stores and map places.
5. Highlight address, landmark, distance, phone, and directions.

### 3. Product confidence — 90 seconds

1. Open Search.
2. Search for a product or choose a category.
3. Show the ₹ price and sample/live label.
4. Open a product.
5. Show stock, sizes, materials, and store.
6. Save the item and open the store page.

### 4. Retailer workflow — 90 seconds

1. Switch to the seller role.
2. Open Inventory.
3. Add a product.
4. Upload a product image.
5. Show AI-assisted draft content.
6. Edit the draft and explain seller control.

### 5. Close with the funding story — 45 seconds

Say:

> “The next step is density. We want to seed one launch city, onboard a focused
> network of fashion retailers, and measure discovery-to-call, discovery-to-
> directions, and store-visit intent. Funding accelerates the retailer network,
> durable catalog infrastructure, and the local conversion loop.”

## Recovery lines

### If live inventory is unavailable

> “This deployment is showing the clearly labeled sample catalog. The product
> experience remains demonstrable; live retailer inventory is a separate data
> source that we will activate as sellers onboard.”

### If location is denied

> “The app does not invent a city. I can enable location or continue into the
> catalog and demonstrate the same product/store flow.”

### If an external map place has no phone number

> “That field depends on the map provider’s published data. Registered sellers
> can provide verified phone and inventory details directly.”
